fix(leads,business): make SENT history showable and clone a usable container

Two invariants the schema declared but did not enforce, both found by writing the test that had never
been written.

**A SENT message must have content.** `mark_message_sent` refused an instance with no
`current_version_id` — but `message_versions.content` is `text not null`, so an *empty* body was a
legal version. An instance frozen onto one of those is SENT forever with nothing to display, and a
SENT instance's versions and `current_version_id` are immutable by trigger, so it can never be
repaired. `duplicates.test.ts` had been inserting exactly such a row: a SENT instance with no version
at all.

Enforced twice, deliberately:

- `message_versions_content_not_blank_check` — the durable invariant, covering every write path
  including the MCP `nexus.submit_message_draft` tool.
- `assert_sent_message_has_content()` on `message_instances` — so the refusal happens at the moment
  the state is frozen rather than at the insert that caused it.

A trigger rather than a guard inside `mark_message_sent`: that function is 180 lines of
sequence-advance logic, and `create or replace` would mean duplicating all of it into the new
migration, where the next change to the original would silently diverge.

**A cloned Business must have a usable container.** `cloneBusiness` copied `sequences` rows without
their versions or steps, and a sequence whose `current_version_id` is null has no steps — so every
cloned sequence was an empty shell that could not produce a message. It now copies the published
version and its steps and points the new sequence at them. The sequence is created `draft` on
purpose: a clone has not been reviewed for the new business, and a draft sequence produces nothing
until it is published, but publishing it is now one action rather than a rewrite.

`cloneBusiness` had no test at all. It now has one covering what a clone copies, what it must not
copy (leads, conversations, message history, source evidence), that the clone is audited with its
source, and that a duplicate key is refused.
